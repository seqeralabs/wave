Wave 1.30.0 was released on November 27, 2025.

## Mandatory steps

Wave 1.30.0 moves the default BuildKit image to the Seqera-hosted registry, and makes the init
container image for Singularity builds configurable — it was previously hardcoded to `busybox` from
Docker Hub:

| Setting | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `wave.build.buildkit-image` | `moby/buildkit:v0.25.2-rootless` | `public.cr.seqera.io/wave/buildkit:v0.25.2-rootless` |
| `wave.build.singularity-image-init` | `busybox` (hardcoded) | `public.cr.seqera.io/wave/busybox:latest` |

If your deployment restricts which registries it can pull from, allow `public.cr.seqera.io` — or
mirror both images and point the two settings at your copies. Container builds fail otherwise.
